Keepers Privacy Policy

Keepers by SASK · Published by SFH SASK Rentals LLC · Effective November 14, 2026

The short version: Keepers is a local-first app. Your photos and videos, and the index Keepers builds from them, stay on your own devices. We do not run a cloud service that receives your library, we do not sell personal information, and the app contains no advertising or third-party tracking.

1. What Keepers processes

Keepers analyzes the photo and video libraries you point it at — on the device where the app is installed. It reads file metadata (such as dates, dimensions, and location tags stored in the files) and creates derived data on that device: thumbnails, visual embeddings, duplicate groupings, and face clusters. This derived data is stored on your device, in the app's own data folder. It is your data and your index.

2. What we collect

By default, nothing leaves your device. The core app requires no account, uploads no photos, videos, thumbnails, or index data to us, and includes no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. We have no server-side copy of your library to sell, share, or lose.

3. On-device AI

Keepers' analysis — duplicate detection, image quality scoring, face detection and clustering, and semantic search — runs on your device using local models. Your media is analyzed where it lives.

4. Optional cloud features

Some optional features, where offered, use a cloud AI provider — for example an opt-in cloud-assisted analysis pass, or prompts that go beyond the local model's reach using your own provider API key. These features are off by default and run only when you explicitly enable them. When you do, only the data needed for that feature is sent to the provider you selected, under that provider's privacy policy. For the opt-in cloud-assisted face pass, face crops of children are excluded by default. You can turn these features off at any time; the local features keep working.

5. Your other devices

If you enroll more than one of your own devices in Keepers, the devices can share index information with each other — for example over your home network — so the same library is searchable everywhere and duplicates that exist on several devices are recognized as one item. Your media files themselves stay on the device that holds them, and a file is only ever deleted by the device that owns it, on your instruction.

6. Deleting photos

Keepers never deletes silently. Items you choose to remove are first staged in the app's trash bin, where you can review and restore them. Permanent deletion happens only when you confirm emptying the trash. Libraries on network storage are treated as read-only: Keepers never writes to or deletes from a network share — removing such an item hides it from your Keepers library only.

7. Credentials

Keepers does not store passwords for your network storage or other services. Where a connection needs a credential, it is held by your operating system's keychain, not by Keepers.

8. Children

Keepers is a general-audience tool for managing your own photo library. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Security

Your library data and index live on your devices, protected by your device and operating-system account security. Because the default design keeps your data off our servers, the most sensitive part of the system — your photos — never transits or rests on infrastructure we control.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Material changes to how optional cloud features handle your data will also be described in the app before you enable them.
