WheresDParty Privacy Policy

WheresDParty · Operated by SFH SASK Rentals LLC · Effective November 19, 2026

The short version: WheresDParty is a party and event planning app. We collect the minimum needed to run it — your phone number for sign-in, your profile, and the events you create or join. We use your phone number to send one-time sign-in codes by text message. We do not sell personal information, and we do not send marketing texts without your consent.

1. What we collect

When you use WheresDParty we collect: your mobile phone number (used as your sign-in identity); profile details you provide, such as your name and the role you pick in the app; the events, guest lists, polls, and bookings you create or take part in; and basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as device and app version information and service logs.

2. How we use it

We use this information to operate the app: verifying your phone number at sign-in, showing your events and invitations, connecting hosts, guests, venues, and vendors you choose to interact with, preventing abuse, and improving the service. Your phone number is used to send one-time passcodes and service notifications related to your account and events.

3. Text messages

By entering your phone number in the app and requesting a code, you consent to receive one-time passcode text messages from WheresDParty. Message frequency varies with your sign-in activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. We do not share your phone number with third parties for their marketing.

4. What we share

We share information only as needed to run the service: with cloud service providers that host the app and deliver its messages (for example, our hosting and messaging providers), and with the hosts, guests, venues, and vendors involved in events you create or join, limited to what those interactions require. We may disclose information if required by law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Retention

We keep account information while your account is active and delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed for the purposes above, subject to legal obligations. You may ask us to delete your account and associated personal information by contacting us at the address below.

6. Security

We protect personal information with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to its nature, including encryption in transit and access controls on our systems.

7. Children

WheresDParty is intended for adults planning and attending social events. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
