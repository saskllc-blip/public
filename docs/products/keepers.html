Product

Keepers

On-device local LLM photo cleanup and organization. Your photos never leave the house — they are never uploaded to us. Keep the memories; skip the cloud photo subscription bill.

Keepers indexes your library where it already lives. It does not copy your photos into a cloud account. Analysis runs on your device with local models: organization, face and person grouping, and cleanup decisions you control.

  • Index, don’t copy Library organization that points at your files — your originals stay put.
  • Photos stay local Nothing left the house. By default, no account and no upload of your library.
  • Face & person grouping On-device face detection and clustering so people are grouped where your media lives.
  • Staged trash with restore Nothing deletes silently. Review and restore before permanent removal.
