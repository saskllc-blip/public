The founder

Senior-engineer hands on every project.

KK

Kiran Keshava

Founder & CTO, SASK LLC

Saratoga, California

Connect on LinkedIn

Kiran brings 20+ years of engineering and quality leadership to a two-person-scale studio on purpose: the person who scopes your project is the person who builds it. He currently serves as Director of Engineering, Quality & Automation at Bio-Rad Laboratories, leading software quality for FDA-regulated diagnostic systems — work where “mostly works” is not an option, and the same standard runs SASK’s client work.

Before Bio-Rad he led engineering and quality organizations at Amazon Lab126 (connectivity & software for Echo and Fire TV, ~100 engineers), Apple (Core OS system test), Qualcomm / Atheros (director, WLAN/Bluetooth, 13 years), Dexcom, and Latch (VP, Quality Engineering) — shipping consumer devices, medical software, and carrier infrastructure at scales from startup to millions of units. He is also co-founder of WheresTheParty, an AI-driven event-discovery app.
